Interactive Division

About the Studio

An independent game studio building original titles with the engineering discipline of mission-critical software.

The studio

The Interactive Division is the game-development arm of Unlimited Data Works LLC. The company is how Craig LaForest provides fractional-CTO and technical-leadership services; the Interactive Division is where that same engineering discipline goes into original games.

The studio builds one focused game at a time, caps its scope, and ships it finished. Craig leads the design, the engine technology, and the production.

About the founder

Craig LaForest — Founder & Technical Lead

Craig has spent 25+ years leading technology in mission-critical environments where downtime and defects are not options — carrying a track record of 99.99%+ uptime and systems that stay correct under pressure. That background is the foundation of the studio: a game is a real-time software system, and the same rigor that keeps critical systems running is what makes a game feel solid.

Craig is a hands-on engineer and technical leader. On Grimoire & Grime, that means the GPU dirt-mask tech (brush painting on the GPU, with completion measured from mip-reduced masks and no per-frame CPU readback), the data-driven content pipeline, and the DualSense feel. Each is treated as an engineering problem first.
